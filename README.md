# JS Scientific Glassworks website

This is the first local version of the company website. It is a simple information and gallery website—there is no login, shopping cart, payment or database.

## Open it on your laptop

1. Open the `js-scientific-glassworks` folder.
2. Double-click `index.html`.
3. It will open in your web browser. The layout adapts for phones, tablets and desktop screens; resize the browser or use your phone's browser to check the mobile menu and stacked sections.

## Files you will edit later

- `index.html` — words, phone number, email, address and sections.
- `style.css` — colours, spacing and overall design.
- `assets/` — your workshop and product photographs. Add future photos here, then add them to the Gallery section in `index.html`.

## Before publishing online

Check the company address and all contact details with your father. When the local version is approved, the next lesson is choosing a domain and basic web hosting, then uploading these exact files.
